Frequently Asked Questions

What is Divi and what does it include?

Divi is the world's most popular WordPress theme and visual page builder by Elegant Themes. A membership includes the Divi Theme, Divi Builder plugin, Extra Magazine Theme, Bloom Email Opt-In plugin, and Monarch Social Sharing plugin — all usable on unlimited websites.

Is Divi really free to try for 30 days?

Absolutely. Every Divi membership — yearly or lifetime — allows you to use Divi on as many websites as you like with a single license. There are no per-site fees or restrictions. This makes it ideal for freelancers and agencies.

Can I use Divi on unlimited websites?

Yes. There are no per-site fees, so you can use it on as many sites as you like, including client sites.

What is Divi Pro and what extra benefits does it include?

Divi Pro bundles the core Divi membership with powerful add-on services: Divi AI (unlimited content/image/code generation), Divi Cloud (unlimited asset storage), Divi VIP (≤30 min guaranteed support), and Divi Teams (4 team member seats) — all at 69% off compared to buying individually.
